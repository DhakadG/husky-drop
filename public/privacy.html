Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 9, 2026

LostHusky Drop is a personal media transfer and sharing application used to collect files through private drop links, save them to Google Drive, and share private gallery links with invited viewers. This policy explains what information the application processes and how it is used.

Information we collect

When someone uses a drop link, the application may collect the uploader name they enter, file names, file sizes, MIME types, upload progress, completion status, timestamps, link identifiers, browser/network metadata, and limited diagnostic error details needed to operate the transfer.

When someone uses a private share link with Google sign-in enabled, the application requests the Google OAuth scopes openid, email, and profile. This allows the application to receive the viewer's Google account email address, display name, and profile picture. The application logs share activity such as opening a share, navigating folders, viewing files, scrubbing media, and downloading files so the owner can understand who accessed the private share.

Files are stored in the owner's configured Google Drive account. The application uses Google Drive metadata and media access only to create upload sessions, list selected folders, generate thumbnails where available, stream downloads, and verify completed uploads.

How we use information

  • To create and protect private upload and share links.
  • To save uploaded files into Google Drive folders selected by the owner.
  • To show live transfer progress, queue status, retry state, and upload history.
  • To provide share galleries, media playback, downloads, and ZIP archives.
  • To attribute private share views and downloads to signed-in viewers when enabled.
  • To send owner notifications when notification settings are enabled.
  • To detect abuse, enforce rate limits, diagnose errors, and protect the service.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not ask for broad Google account permissions beyond sign-in identity for viewers and Drive access required by the owner-configured application.
  • We do not intentionally publish private drop or share links; access depends on the link, PIN/sign-in settings, and expiry state configured by the owner.

Storage and processors

Uploaded files and shared Drive content are stored in Google Drive. The application runs on Cloudflare Workers and may store operational metadata in Cloudflare KV and Durable Objects. If email notifications are enabled, notification content may be sent through the configured email provider. These services process data only as needed to provide the application.

Retention

Files remain in Google Drive until the owner deletes them or changes their Drive storage. Link metadata, activity logs, upload history, and live transfer state are retained as needed for the dashboard, security, troubleshooting, and operational history. Short-lived download and ZIP tickets expire automatically.

Security

The application uses HTTPS, signed cookies/tokens, short-lived download links, PIN hashing, rate limiting, expiry checks, and private dashboard authentication. No internet service can be guaranteed perfectly secure, so users should only upload and share content they are authorized to provide.

Your choices

If you are a viewer and do not want your Google name/email associated with share activity, do not continue past the Google sign-in gate. If you are an uploader, ask the link owner to remove uploaded files or associated metadata from their Drive/dashboard records.

Contact
